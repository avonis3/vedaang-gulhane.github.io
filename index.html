About Me

I'm a Product Manager based in Mumbai with a background in Computer Science. I enjoy working at the intersection of data, user empathy, and business outcomes, and feel most at home in teams that care deeply about the details.

At Medikabazaar, I work on the customer experience side of the marketplace, from notifications and retention to checkout and subscriptions. A lot of what I do involves understanding where users drop off and finding ways to serve them better.

Before this, I was part of the founding team at Cipla Digital Health, where we built a healthcare app from scratch. I got to work on a wide range of things, from medicine delivery to payment integrations to app localisation, and learned a lot about what it takes to grow a product to scale.

Outside of work, I wrote a Python book that hit Amazon bestseller, taught coding to students, and ranked 1801 in IIT-JEE Advanced. I enjoy learning new things and figuring out hard problems.
